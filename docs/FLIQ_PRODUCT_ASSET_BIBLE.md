## 1. BRAND DNA

### 1.1 Brand Position

FLIQ is a monolithic, architectural luxury streetwear house formed in Madhapur, Hyderabad. Positioned at the intersection of high fashion editorial minimalism and tactical urban utilitarianism, FLIQ rejects generic e-commerce trends in favor of heavy-weight draping, uncompromised raw materials, and sculpted silhouettes.

### 1.2 Brand Philosophy

- **Form Follows Monolith**: Every silhouette is treated as a piece of brutalist architecture.
- **Uncompromised Substance**: Heavyweight cotton weaves (300GSM – 500GSM), 14oz selvedge denim, and hydrophobic technical canvases override fast fashion shortcuts.
- **Monochromatic Discipline**: Color palettes are rooted in deep obsidian, graphite, off-white, olive twill, dark indigo, and charcoal.

### 1.3 Target Audience

Creative directors, architects, tech innovators, luxury connoisseurs, and streetwear purists aged 20–42 seeking sculptural everyday garments that communicate quiet authority, structural integrity, and architectural permanence.

### 1.4 Visual Identity

- **Primary Aesthetics**: Deep black glass environment, matte metallic highlights, high-contrast typography, generous whitespace.
- **Typography Standard**: Classical serif headers (`FLIQ`) juxtaposed with modern geometric sans-serif sub-elements (`UNISEX`, `DROP 01`).
- **Color Discipline**: `#000000` (Pure Black Base), `#08080a` (Obsidian), `#18181b` (Zinc-900), `#e4e4e7` (Metallic Chrome Accent), `#ffffff` (Pure White).

### 1.5 Editorial Tone & Fashion Language

- **Tone**: Authoritative, precise, architectural, unhurried, luxury-bound.
- **Key Terms**: _Monolithic Silhouette_, _Heavyweight Weave_, _Tactical Articulation_, _Brutalist Tailoring_, _Hydrophobic Canvas_, _Selvedge Edge_.

## 4. PHOTOGRAPHY RULES

### 4.1 Lighting Specification

- **Primary Setup**: Single high-key overhead softbox combined with a 45-degree hard rim light to emphasize fabric grain and shoulder architecture.
- **Color Temperature**: 5600K (Clean Daylight Balanced).
- **Contrast**: High key contrast with deep crushed shadows (`#000000`) and preserved specular highlights on hardware.

### 4.2 Camera & Lens Standard

- **Camera Body**: Medium Format Digital (Hasselblad H6D-100c or Phase One XF).
- **Lenses**: 120mm Macro Lens for Fabric & Construction views; 85mm Prime Lens for Editorial & Hero views.
- **Aperture**: `f/8.0` for complete garment clarity; `f/2.8` for cinematic depth-of-field editorial shots.

### 4.3 Background & Setting

- **Background**: Seamless matte black studio backdrop (`#000000`) with polished black glass floor reflections.
- **Environment**: Zero studio props, zero distraction, pure space.

### 4.4 Color Grading & Editing Style

- **Color Profile**: Desaturated neutrals with enhanced deep black values.
- **Texture Sharpness**: Unsharp mask tuned specifically for cotton weaves, nylon sheen, and denim twill lines.
- **Color Accuracy**: Absolute fidelity to garment swatches (Zero hue distortion).

## 6. CONSISTENCY RULES

### 6.1 Garment Identity Rules

1. **Identical Hardware**: The zipper pullers, buttons, drawstrings, and metal rivets depicted in View 01 MUST match View 02, View 03, and View 04 without variation.
2. **Identical Fabric Weave**: A 500GSM French Terry hoodie MUST show identical fleece loop density across all views.
3. **Identical Branding**: Logo scale (e.g., 12mm back collar micro-embroidery) MUST remain perfectly consistent across all angles.
4. **Zero Footwear or Accessories**: No shoes, caps, bags, or jewelry are permitted in product shots or cards.
